# House of Cinders

**Short description:** Draft a trio. Chain nine elements. Defeat ten bosses—or take the Infinity Key into endless Hell.

Build your opening hand from four random heroes, then step into a real-time isometric boss arena. Switch between up to three heroes, combine elemental skills, and dodge attacks before their warning shapes turn deadly.

Every victory opens the Night Market: recruit allies, trade weapons, and build a team around powerful reactions. Clear the ten-floor House to claim the Infinity Key. Cash out your victory—or enter Hell, where familiar bosses return with new combinations and rising difficulty.

- 18 heroes across four rarities and nine elements.
- Ten distinct bosses, ten Hell variants, and endless scaling.
- Eight weapons, twelve Curios, and three consumables.
- A short two-fight demo, autosave, remappable actions, adjustable audio, large text, and reduced motion.

## Controls

| Action | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Move | WASD / arrows |
| Aim / basic attack | Mouse / left click |
| Elemental skill | E / right click |
| Dodge | Space |
| Switch heroes | 1 / 2 / 3 |
| Consumables | Q / R |
| Pause / close | Esc |
| Help / settings | F1 / F2 |
| Navigate menus / activate | Tab or arrows / Enter |

Click the game to focus it. Music starts after your first input. Combine two different elemental skills to trigger a reaction; off-field heroes recover cooldowns but do not attack. Floor clears revive and heal the team.

## Browser and save notes

Desktop keyboard and mouse required. Chromium and Firefox tested on Linux; Safari, mobile touch and gamepad support are not verified. Progress and settings stay in this browser. Reloading during a fight restores its entry snapshot. Private mode, blocked storage, or clearing site data may prevent persistence; a warning appears when storage is unavailable. There is no cloud save or account requirement.
