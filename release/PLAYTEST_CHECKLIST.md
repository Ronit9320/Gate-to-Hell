# Human acceptance record

Automated evidence is recorded in HANDOFF.md and PHASE_AUDIT.md. These checks require an actual player; they have not been completed by automation.

Record tester, date, version/checksum, browser/device, outcome and any issue for each:

- [ ] A new player completes the two-fight demo and explains switching, elemental reactions and shop recruitment.
- [ ] A player completes a normal ten-floor run, saves/reloads, buys/sells/equips/replaces heroes, enters Hell, loses and restarts. Check both standard and large text.
- [ ] Clear all ten normal bosses and ten Hell variants without invulnerability; assess warning readability and safe routes. The source-only F3 lab can select encounters; never enable cheats for acceptance clears.
- [ ] Review early and late Hell pacing. Expert simulation is not a human duration estimate; the initial 20–35-minute normal-run goal is unverified.
- [ ] Listen to the music and all sound cues; check mute, master/music/effects controls, pause and focus loss. Automated audio-start detection does not establish audible quality.
- [ ] Check the final artwork and credits, then recapture cover/screenshots.
- [ ] Test the actual itch.io iframe on supported desktop browsers. Confirm jam rules and submission status separately.

Known release limitations: geometric placeholders by author request; desktop keyboard/mouse only; no cloud save; mid-fight reload starts the encounter again; headless performance measurements do not guarantee 60 FPS on every device. Safari and mobile are unverified. Full manual acceptance and jam-specific compliance remain open.
